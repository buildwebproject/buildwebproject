## Hi, we're BuildWebProject 👋

A **Sylius e-commerce studio from India**. We build complete online stores on [Sylius](https://sylius.com) (Symfony), and sell the plugins and storefront themes we created for them, so brands can **own their store, their data and their code**, with no per-order platform fee.

### What we do

- 🛒 **Complete Sylius stores**: catalog, payments, couriers, GST invoices, WhatsApp, admin. Staging → production workflow, daily off-site backups.
- 🧩 **Sylius plugins**: Razorpay, Smart COD, Delhivery, Shiprocket/NimbusPost, WhatsApp Commerce + AI assistant, AI voice COD confirmation, Zoho Books, GST invoicing, POS, returns, Meilisearch and more.
- 🎨 **Storefront themes**: fast, mobile-first Sylius 2 themes that keep native Twig Hooks and checkout.
- 🔁 **Migrations** from Shopify, WooCommerce and other platforms.

### Live work

**[Unjha Masala Mart](https://unjhamasalamart.com)**: spice & grocery brand selling across India. Sylius 2.3 · Symfony 7.4 · 10 languages · UPI + COD · Delhivery · WhatsApp AI · Zoho Books · POS.

### Stack

`Sylius 2` · `Symfony 7` · `PHP 8.3` · `MySQL` · `Meilisearch` · `Twig Hooks` · `Stimulus` · `Astro` · `Cloudflare`
